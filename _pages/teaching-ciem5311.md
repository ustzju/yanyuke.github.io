Course Syllabus
======

### Topic 1: Typhoon and Wind Environment

Global wind climate; wind climate in Hong Kong; parent and extreme wind analysis; design wind speed and risk; wind structures near ground; wind speed and turbulence; engineering models for wind speed profiles. Typhoon damages; typhoon categorization and warning system; typhoon boundary layer dynamics; typhoon numerical forecast; typhoon wind field model; Monte Carlo simulation.

### Topic 2: Wind Pressure and Building Aerodynamics

Introduction to wind pressure; wind pressure on rectangular buildings; building aerodynamics; flow around buildings; wind forces and moments.

### Topic 3: Structural Dynamics and Wind-Induced Vibrations

Formulation of equation of motion; undamped and damped free vibration; harmonic excitation; periodic excitation; random vibration; spectral analysis. Along-wind response of structures; cross-wind response of structures; interference excitation of tall buildings.

### Topic 4: Wind Loading Codes, Wind Tunnel Testing

Code of Practice of Wind Effects in Hong Kong 2004 & 2019; Australian/New Zealand Standard, Structural Design Actions Part 2: Wind Actions 2021. Principles of wind tunnel tests; wind tunnel tests for building aerodynamics.
