Course Syllabus
======

- Newton's laws of motion, inertial reference frames
- Dynamics of single-degree-of-freedom systems
  - Free and forced vibration, effects of damping
  - Fourier series and Duhamel integration methods
- Dynamics of multiple-degree-of-freedom systems
  - Stiffness and mass matrices
  - Rayleigh damping
  - Normal mode analysis, eigenvalues and eigenvectors
- Dynamics of continuous systems
  - Longitudinal vibration of rods
  - Transverse vibration of beams
- Special topics
  - Hamilton's principle and Lagrange's equations
  - Energy approach and applications
  - Tuned mass dampers
  - Earthquake response spectra
